# A2 · Unit 02 — Travel & holidays

**Can-do:** I can describe what was happening at a moment in the past and set the background to a story about a trip.

## Grammar focus
Past continuous, and past continuous vs past simple. Form the past continuous with *was / were + verb-ing*. Use it for an action in progress at a past moment, or for the longer background action that another (shorter) action interrupts. The interrupting action goes in the past simple, often joined with *when*; two parallel background actions are joined with *while*.

- At 8 p.m. we **were flying** over the sea.
- They **weren't sleeping** when the plane landed.
- **Was** she **waiting** at the airport?
- I **was packing** my suitcase **when** the taxi **arrived**.
- **While** he **was sunbathing**, it **started** to rain.
- We **were** tired, so we **stayed** at the hotel.
- What **were** you **doing** at midnight?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
journey — travel from one place to another
suitcase — a large bag for clothes when you travel
luggage — the bags you take on a trip
passport — an official document you need to travel abroad
abroad — in or to another country
flight — a journey by plane
beach — the sand beside the sea
coast — the land next to the sea
hotel — a building where you pay to sleep on a trip
tourist — a person who visits a place on holiday
pack — to put things into a bag or case
sunbathe — to sit or lie in the sun

## Functions & phrases
- Describing a trip: "We were staying near the beach.", "The weather was lovely."
- Setting the scene: "It was getting dark when…", "While we were driving,…"
- Asking about a holiday: "Where were you staying?", "What was the weather like?"
- Reacting: "That sounds amazing.", "How relaxing!"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short holiday postcard or blog entry describing a scene in progress.
- conversation: role-play telling a friend about a trip that went wrong (interrupted by an event).
- quiz focus: choosing past continuous for background vs past simple for the interrupting action.

## Watch out
- When I arrived, she cooked dinner → When I arrived, she was cooking dinner
- We was waiting for the bus → We were waiting for the bus
- While we were walk in the city → While we were walking in the city
