# A2 · Unit 01 — My story

**Can-do:** I can narrate a short sequence of past events in my life and ask and answer simple questions about when things happened.

## Grammar focus
Past simple (regular and irregular). Use the past simple for finished actions at a definite time in the past. Regular verbs add *-ed* (work → worked, live → lived); many common verbs are irregular (go → went, have → had, see → saw). The form is the same for all persons. Negatives and questions use *did / didn't* with the base verb, so the main verb loses its past ending. Time expressions like *yesterday, last week, in 2019, two years ago* usually go at the end.

- I **studied** English at school.
- She **went** to Canada last summer.
- We **didn't have** a car when I was young.
- He **didn't see** the message.
- **Did** you **live** in Madrid?
- **When did** they **meet**?
- I **was** born in a small town.
- They **were** very happy that day.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
born — starting your life
grow up — to change from a child into an adult
childhood — the time when you were a child
move — to go and live in a new place
memory — something you keep in your mind from the past
happen — to take place
meet — to see and speak to someone for the first time
leave — to go away from a place or person
become — to start to be something
ago — before now, in the past
last — the one before this one (last week, last year)
diary — a book where you write about your days

## Functions & phrases
- Telling a life story: "I was born in…", "I grew up in…", "Later, we moved to…"
- Saying when: "Two years ago…", "When I was ten…", "In 2015…"
- Asking about the past: "Where did you grow up?", "When did that happen?"
- Reacting: "Really?", "That's interesting.", "What happened next?"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short first-person biography (5–6 sentences) of an ordinary person's early life.
- conversation: partners interview each other about their childhood and where they grew up.
- quiz focus: choosing regular vs irregular past forms; forming *did*-questions and *didn't*-negatives.

## Watch out
- I didn't went → I didn't go
- Where you went yesterday? → Where did you go yesterday?
- She borned in 1990 → She was born in 1990
