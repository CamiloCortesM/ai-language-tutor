# A2 · Unit 04 — Future plans

**Can-do:** I can talk about my intentions, make predictions, and offer to do things, choosing the right future form.

## Grammar focus
*be going to* vs *will*. Use *be going to* for plans and intentions decided before now, and for predictions based on what you can see. Use *will* for predictions or beliefs about the future, for offers and promises, and for quick decisions made at the moment of speaking. Present continuous is also common for fixed arrangements with a time and place.

- I'**m going to** look for a new job next month.
- Look at those clouds — it'**s going to** rain.
- I think she'**ll** pass the exam.
- The bags are heavy — I'**ll** carry one for you.
- **Will** you **help** me later?
- We'**re meeting** the manager at 3 p.m. tomorrow.
- They **aren't going to** move this year.
- **What are** you **going to** do at the weekend?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
plan — an idea about what you will do
decide — to choose after thinking
intend — to plan to do something
promise — to say you will certainly do something
offer — to say you are willing to do something for someone
hope — to want something good to happen
probably — very likely, almost certainly
maybe — perhaps, possibly
soon — in a short time from now
arrangement — a plan you make with other people
retire — to stop working near the end of your life
save — to keep money to use later

## Functions & phrases
- Stating intentions: "I'm going to start a course.", "We're planning to travel."
- Predicting: "I think it will be fine.", "You'll probably like it."
- Offering/promising: "I'll do it for you.", "I promise I'll call."
- Arranging: "Are you free on Friday?", "Let's meet at six."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short message where someone describes weekend plans and a prediction.
- conversation: two friends make an arrangement to meet, deciding day, time and place.
- quiz focus: choosing *going to* (plan/evidence) vs *will* (prediction/offer/decision).

## Watch out
- I will buy a car next year (already decided) → I'm going to buy a car next year
- Wait, I'm going to help you now → Wait, I'll help you now
- I hope I will to pass → I hope I'll pass
