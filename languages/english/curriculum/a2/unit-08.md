# A2 · Unit 08 — Getting around

**Can-do:** I can explain why I do things, describe how actions are done, and ask for and give directions.

## Grammar focus
Infinitive of purpose and adverbs of manner. Use the *to + verb* infinitive to say **why** you do something — it answers "What for?" (do not use *for + verb*). Adverbs of manner say **how** an action is done; most are made by adding *-ly* to the adjective (slow → slowly, careful → carefully), some change spelling (easy → easily), and a few are irregular (good → well, fast → fast). They usually come after the verb or after the object.

- I went to the station **to buy** a ticket.
- She's saving money **to travel** next year.
- We took a taxi **to get** there faster.
- He drives **carefully** in the city.
- Please speak **slowly**.
- The train left **early**.
- They finished the trip **safely**.
- She speaks English very **well**.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
underground — the city railway that runs below the ground
platform — the place where you wait for a train
fare — the money you pay for a journey
traffic — the vehicles moving on a road
corner — the point where two streets meet
roundabout — a circular junction where roads meet
pedestrian — a person walking in the street
lane — a narrow road, or one part of a wide road
direction — the way to a place (left, right, straight on)
straight — not turning to the left or right
slowly — at a low speed
safely — without danger or harm

## Functions & phrases
- Asking directions: "How do I get to the station?", "Is it far from here?"
- Giving directions: "Go straight on.", "Turn left at the corner.", "It's on your right."
- Explaining purpose: "I'm here to catch the 9 o'clock train."
- Describing how: "Drive carefully.", "The bus came quickly."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short set of directions from a station to a hotel.
- conversation: role-play stopping a stranger to ask the way across a town map.
- quiz focus: *to + verb* for purpose, and forming adverbs of manner correctly.

## Watch out
- I came here for buy a ticket → I came here to buy a ticket
- He drives very careful → He drives very carefully
- She speaks English very good → She speaks English very well
