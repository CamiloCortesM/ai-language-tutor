# A2 · Unit 09 — People, then & now

**Can-do:** I can describe how people and habits were different in the past, and describe someone's appearance and character.

## Grammar focus
*used to*, and describing appearance and personality. Use *used to + base verb* for past habits and past states that are no longer true. The form is the same for all persons; questions and negatives use *did / didn't use to* (drop the *-d*). It contrasts a past situation with the present, so it often pairs with "…but now…".

- I **used to** have long hair.
- We **used to** live near the sea.
- He **didn't use to** like vegetables.
- **Did** you **use to** play a sport?
- She **used to** be very shy, but now she's confident.
- They **used to** walk to school every day.
- My grandfather **used to** tell great stories.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
appearance — the way a person looks
personality — the kind of person someone is inside
curly — with lots of natural curves (used for hair)
bald — having little or no hair on the head
beard — the hair that grows on a man's chin and cheeks
moustache — the hair a man grows above his lip
polite — behaving in a pleasant, respectful way
shy — nervous about meeting or talking to people
generous — happy to give and share with others
honest — always telling the truth
lazy — not wanting to work or make an effort
attractive — pleasant to look at

## Functions & phrases
- Talking about the past: "I used to…", "When I was younger, I…"
- Describing looks: "She's got curly hair.", "He's tall with a short beard."
- Describing character: "He's very generous.", "She can be a bit shy."
- Comparing then and now: "I used to be lazy, but now I'm quite active."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short "how life has changed" text contrasting past habits with today.
- conversation: describe a family member's appearance and personality to a partner.
- quiz focus: *used to* forms (positive/negative/question) and appearance/personality adjectives.

## Watch out
- I use to play football when I was young → I used to play football when I was young
- Did you used to live here? → Did you use to live here?
- She has curly hairs → She has curly hair
