# A2 · Unit 12 — Milestone

**Can-do:** I can handle the everyday A2 tasks of the whole level — narrating, comparing, planning, advising and describing — accurately enough to pass an A2 Key-style checkpoint.

## Grammar focus
Full A2 review (no new grammar). This unit brings together every criterial structure from Units 1–11: past simple and past continuous; comparatives, superlatives and *as … as*; *be going to* vs *will*; *should* and *have to / must*; quantifiers with *too / enough*; present perfect with *ever/never/just/already/yet* vs past simple; infinitive of purpose and adverbs of manner; *used to*; zero and first conditional with *must / mustn't / don't have to*; and verb + gerund/infinitive with connectors. Mix them as real communication does.

- Last year I **went** to Italy, and while I **was travelling** I **met** new friends.
- This café is **cheaper**, so I think it'**s going to** be popular.
- You **should** rest, but you **don't have to** stay in bed.
- I'**ve just** finished the course — I **used to** find English hard.
- **If** you practise every day, you'**ll improve**.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
checkpoint — a point where your progress is tested
revise — to study something again before a test
practise — to do something regularly to get better at it
improve — to become better
progress — the process of getting better over time
skill — an ability you develop by learning and practice
confident — sure that you can do something well
fluent — able to speak a language smoothly and easily
mistake — something you do wrongly
correct — right, with no mistakes
goal — something you are trying to achieve
achieve — to succeed in reaching a goal

## Functions & phrases
- Reflecting: "I've improved a lot.", "I used to make this mistake, but now I don't."
- Setting goals: "My goal is to become fluent.", "I'm going to revise every day."
- Managing an exam exchange: "Could you repeat that, please?", "Let me think for a moment."
- Encouraging: "You're doing well.", "Just stay confident."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a mixed short text (message + short article) with questions across all A2 grammar.
- conversation: a paired task like A2 Key Speaking — an interview plus a collaborative decision using a visual prompt.
- quiz focus: an **A2 Key (KET)-style checkpoint** covering Units 1–11 — a Reading & Writing section (matching, multiple choice, gap-fill, a ~25-word message), a short Listening, and a paired Speaking task; pass mark ≥70%, including the spoken part.

## Watch out
- I have finished the course last month → I finished the course last month
- If you will practise, you improve → If you practise, you'll improve
- I want improving my English → I want to improve my English
