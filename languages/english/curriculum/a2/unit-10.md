# A2 · Unit 10 — Rules & possibilities

**Can-do:** I can talk about rules, general truths, and real future possibilities and their results.

## Grammar focus
Zero and first conditional, plus *must / mustn't / don't have to*. The **zero conditional** (*If + present simple, present simple*) states facts and general truths that are always true. The **first conditional** (*If + present simple, will + base verb*) talks about a real or likely future situation and its result; *if* and *when* can both introduce the condition. For rules: *must* = necessary, *mustn't* = not allowed, *don't have to* = not necessary.

- **If** you heat ice, it **melts**.
- **If** it **rains** tomorrow, we'**ll stay** at home.
- She'**ll be** angry **if** you **arrive** late.
- **When** the light **turns** red, cars **stop**.
- You **must** wear a seatbelt.
- You **mustn't** park here.
- You **don't have to** pay to enter.
- What **will** you **do if** they **say** no?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
rule — a thing you must or must not do
allowed — permitted, that you may do
forbidden — not permitted, that you must not do
permission — being allowed to do something
fine — money you pay for breaking a rule
warning — words that tell you about a possible danger
sign — a notice that gives you information or an order
litter — rubbish left in a public place
behave — to act in a particular way
dangerous — likely to cause harm
safe — not dangerous, free from harm
notice — a written sign giving information

## Functions & phrases
- Stating rules: "You must show your ticket.", "You mustn't use your phone here."
- Reassuring: "You don't have to pay in advance."
- Talking possibilities: "If we leave now, we'll catch the bus."
- Warning: "Be careful — if you touch it, it'll break."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short list of park or library rules with signs.
- conversation: agree on five rules for a shared flat using *must / mustn't / don't have to*.
- quiz focus: zero vs first conditional forms, and *mustn't* vs *don't have to*.

## Watch out
- If it will rain, we stay home → If it rains, we'll stay home
- You mustn't to park here → You mustn't park here
- You don't must be late → You mustn't be late
