# A2 · Unit 06 — Food & restaurants

**Can-do:** I can talk about quantities of food, order a meal, and say whether there is too much, too little or enough of something.

## Grammar focus
Quantifiers, *too / enough*, and *would like* review. Use *much* with uncountable nouns and *many* with countable nouns, mostly in questions and negatives; *a lot of* works with both, usually in positive sentences. Use *(a) few* with countable nouns and *(a) little* with uncountable nouns. *too + adjective* means "more than you want"; *enough* means "as much as you need" (it goes after adjectives but before nouns). *would like* is a polite way to ask for or offer something.

- There isn't **much** salt in the soup.
- How **many** dishes did you order?
- We ate **a lot of** vegetables.
- I have **a few** friends coming for dinner.
- There's only **a little** milk left.
- This coffee is **too** hot.
- We don't have **enough** chairs.
- I'**d like** the fish, please.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
menu — a list of the food a restaurant offers
waiter — a person who brings your food
bill — the paper that shows what you must pay
dish — food prepared in a particular way
starter — the small first course of a meal
dessert — sweet food served at the end of a meal
vegetarian — a person who does not eat meat
delicious — very pleasant to eat
order — to ask for food in a restaurant
recipe — instructions for cooking a dish
flavour — the particular taste of a food
tip — extra money you give for good service

## Functions & phrases
- Ordering: "I'd like the soup to start.", "Can I have the bill, please?"
- Talking quantity: "There's too much sugar.", "We haven't got enough bread."
- Recommending: "You should try the dessert.", "The fish is delicious here."
- Being polite: "Would you like anything to drink?", "Just a little, thanks."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short restaurant menu plus a customer's order.
- conversation: role-play ordering a full meal, then asking for the bill.
- quiz focus: choosing *much/many/a lot of/(a) few/(a) little* and *too/enough*.

## Watch out
- How much dishes do you want? → How many dishes do you want?
- The tea is enough hot → The tea is hot enough
- I like the fish, please (in a restaurant) → I'd like the fish, please
