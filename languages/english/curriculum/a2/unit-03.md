# A2 · Unit 03 — Comparing things

**Can-do:** I can compare two or more people, places or objects and say which is the best or worst.

## Grammar focus
Comparatives, superlatives and *as … as*. Short adjectives add *-er / -est* (cheap → cheaper → the cheapest); adjectives ending in *-y* change to *-ier / -iest* (easy → easier). Long adjectives use *more / most* (more modern, the most popular). Some are irregular: good → better → best, bad → worse → worst. Use *than* after a comparative, *the* before a superlative, and *(not) as … as* to say two things are (or aren't) equal.

- This hotel is **cheaper than** the other one.
- Today is **hotter than** yesterday.
- It's **the most popular** café in town.
- She's **the best** student in the class.
- My phone is **more modern than** yours.
- This street isn't **as quiet as** my old one.
- The blue bag is **as expensive as** the red one.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
cheap — not costing much money
expensive — costing a lot of money
modern — new in style or design
ancient — very old, from long ago
popular — liked by many people
crowded — full of people
comfortable — pleasant and easy to use
similar — almost the same
different — not the same
size — how big or small something is
quality — how good or bad something is
value — how much something is worth for its price

## Functions & phrases
- Comparing: "This one is bigger than that one.", "It's not as good as I thought."
- Choosing: "I think the second one is the best.", "Which is cheaper?"
- Giving reasons: "…because it's more comfortable.", "…since it's less crowded."
- Agreeing/disagreeing: "You're right.", "I don't agree — this one is better."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short product or city comparison text (two options side by side).
- conversation: partners compare two cities, phones or restaurants and agree on the best.
- quiz focus: forming comparatives/superlatives correctly, including irregulars and *as … as*.

## Watch out
- This is more cheaper → This is cheaper
- It's the most big city → It's the biggest city
- She is tall as her brother → She is as tall as her brother
