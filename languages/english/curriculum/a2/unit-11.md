# A2 · Unit 11 — Entertainment & media

**Can-do:** I can say what I enjoy and want to do in my free time and connect ideas in the right order.

## Grammar focus
Verb + gerund / infinitive patterns (introduction) and connectors *so / then / before / after*. Some verbs are followed by the *-ing* form (enjoy, like, love, hate, finish, mind), and others by the *to* infinitive (want, hope, decide, need, would like). A few common verbs allow both with little change of meaning (start, begin, like). Use *so* to show a result, *then* for the next event, and *before / after* to order two events in time.

- I **enjoy watching** documentaries.
- She **wants to see** the new comedy.
- We **finished watching** the series last night.
- They **decided to stay** in and relax.
- It was late, **so** we turned off the TV.
- First we had dinner, **then** we watched a film.
- **Before** the show starts, I'll make popcorn.
- We talked about it **after** we saw it.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
comedy — a film or show that makes you laugh
documentary — a film or programme about real facts
audience — the people who watch or listen to a show
channel — a television or radio station
series — a set of programmes with the same characters
actor — a person who performs in films or plays
scene — one separate part of a film or play
review — a written opinion about a film, book or show
headline — the title of a news story
subtitle — the words shown on screen for what people say
broadcast — to send out a programme on TV or radio
entertaining — enjoyable and fun to watch

## Functions & phrases
- Talking about tastes: "I love watching comedies.", "I don't mind reading reviews."
- Making plans: "Do you want to see a film?", "I'd like to watch that series."
- Ordering ideas: "First we'll eat, then we'll watch it."
- Giving opinions: "It was really entertaining.", "The acting was great."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short film or series review with an opinion and a recommendation.
- conversation: partners recommend a film or show and explain why they enjoy it.
- quiz focus: choosing gerund vs infinitive after common verbs, and connectors *so/then/before/after*.

## Watch out
- I enjoy to watch films → I enjoy watching films
- We decided watching a film → We decided to watch a film
- After we will finish, we go out → After we finish, we'll go out
