# A1 · Unit 06 — Places in town

**Can-do:** Can say where places are, ask for and give simple directions, and talk about what I can and can't do.

## Grammar focus
Prepositions of place, *can* (ability/permission), and imperatives for directions. Common prepositions: **in, on, under, next to, near, between, opposite.** Use **can** + base verb for ability or permission (no *-s*, no *to*): *I can swim. Can I sit here?* Negative is **can't**: *She can't drive.* Imperatives use the base verb with no subject and are useful for directions: *Go straight. Turn left. Take the first street.*

- The bank is next to the park.
- My house is opposite the school.
- Can you help me? — Yes, I can.
- I can't find the station.
- She can speak English.
- Go straight and turn right.
- Take the second street on the left.
- The shop is near the corner.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
street — a road in a town with houses and shops
shop — a place where you buy things
bank — a place that keeps and gives money
park — a green public place with trees
station — a place where trains or buses stop
hospital — a place where sick people go
restaurant — a place where you eat a meal out
corner — the point where two streets meet
left — the opposite side of right
right — the opposite side of left
near — close to a place
between — in the middle of two things

## Functions & phrases
- Asking directions: "Excuse me, where is the bank?" / "How do I get to the park?"
- Giving directions: "Go straight on." / "Turn left at the corner."
- Talking about ability: "I can walk there." / "She can't drive."
- Asking permission: "Can I sit here?" / "Can we go now?"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short set of directions from one place in town to another
- conversation: role-play a tourist asking a local for directions to a station or shop
- quiz focus: prepositions of place, can/can't for ability and permission, and imperative directions

## Watch out
- She cans swim → She can swim
- I can to drive → I can drive
- The park is in front the school → The park is opposite/in front of the school
