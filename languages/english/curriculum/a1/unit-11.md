# A1 · Unit 11 — Yesterday

**Can-do:** Can talk about where I was and how I felt in the past using was/were and simple past time expressions.

## Grammar focus
*Was / were* — the past of *to be*. Use **was** for I/he/she/it and **were** for you/we/they: *I was tired. They were at home.* Negatives: **wasn't / weren't** (*I wasn't late. We weren't ready.*). Questions put the verb first: *Was she happy? Were you at work?* This is the bridge toward the A2 past simple, so pair it with past time expressions: **yesterday, last night, last week, two days ago, then.**

- I was at home yesterday.
- She was very happy.
- We were tired last night.
- He wasn't at work.
- They weren't late.
- Was the film good? — Yes, it was.
- Were you busy yesterday? — No, I wasn't.
- Where were you last week?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
yesterday — the day before today
ago — before now (e.g. two days ago)
last — the most recent (last week, last night)
before — earlier than something
then — at that time; next
happy — feeling good and pleased
sad — feeling unhappy
tired — needing rest or sleep
busy — having a lot to do
late — after the right time
early — before the usual or right time
holiday — a day or time with no work

## Functions & phrases
- Talking about the past: "I was at home." / "We were on holiday."
- Saying when: "yesterday" / "last week" / "two days ago"
- Describing past feelings: "I was tired." / "She was very happy."
- Asking about the past: "Where were you?" / "Was it good?"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short diary entry about where someone was and how they felt yesterday
- conversation: ask a partner about their weekend — where they were and how they felt
- quiz focus: was vs. were, negatives and questions, and matching past time expressions

## Watch out
- I were at home → I was at home
- They was happy → They were happy
- Yesterday I am tired → Yesterday I was tired
