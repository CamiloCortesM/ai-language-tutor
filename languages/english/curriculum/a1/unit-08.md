# A1 · Unit 08 — Free time & hobbies

**Can-do:** Can say what I am doing right now and talk about my hobbies and free-time activities.

## Grammar focus
Present continuous, and a first look at present continuous vs. present simple. Form the present continuous with **am/is/are + verb-ing**: *I am reading. She is running.* Use it for actions happening **now** or around now. Negatives add **not** (*isn't reading*); questions put *be* first (*Are you playing?*). Compare: the **present simple** is for habits and routines (*I play football on Sundays*), while the **present continuous** is for what is happening at this moment (*I am playing football now*).

- I am watching a film.
- She is listening to music.
- We are not playing now.
- He isn't reading.
- Are you dancing? — Yes, I am.
- What are they doing? — They're swimming.
- I usually read in the evening. (routine)
- I am reading a good book now. (now)

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
read — to look at words and understand them
play — to do a game or a sport for fun
run — to move fast on your feet
swim — to move through water
dance — to move your body to music
sing — to make music with your voice
watch — to look at something for a time
listen — to give attention to sound
football — a popular sport with a ball and two teams
music — sounds made by voices or instruments
book — pages with words that you read
game — an activity you play for fun with rules

## Functions & phrases
- Saying what's happening now: "I'm reading." / "She's cooking dinner."
- Asking what someone is doing: "What are you doing?" / "Are you working?"
- Talking about hobbies: "I like playing football." / "In my free time I read."
- Comparing now and usually: "I usually run, but today I'm swimming."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short message describing what a family is doing right now at home
- conversation: describe a picture where several people are doing different activities
- quiz focus: forming the present continuous, and choosing simple vs. continuous by time signal

## Watch out
- I am play football → I am playing football
- She reading a book → She is reading a book
- What you are doing? → What are you doing?
