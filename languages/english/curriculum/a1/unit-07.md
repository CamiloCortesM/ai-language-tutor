# A1 · Unit 07 — Work & study

**Can-do:** Can ask and answer wh- questions about jobs and study, and say dates.

## Grammar focus
Present simple *wh-* questions, plus dates and ordinal numbers. Wh- questions ask for information and start with a question word (**what, where, when, who, why, how**). The pattern is: **wh- word + do/does + subject + base verb**: *Where do you work? What does she do?* For *to be*, put the verb after the wh- word: *What is your job?* Ordinal numbers name positions and dates (**first, second, third, fourth…**): *the first of May, the twenty-third of July.*

- What do you do? — I'm a nurse.
- Where does he work? — He works in an office.
- When do you start work?
- Who is your boss?
- Why do you study English?
- How does she go to work?
- My birthday is on the third of March.
- Today is the twenty-first of June.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
doctor — a person who helps sick people
nurse — a person who cares for patients
driver — a person who drives a car, bus or truck
cook — a person who makes food in a kitchen
waiter — a person who brings food in a restaurant
farmer — a person who grows food or keeps animals
engineer — a person who designs or builds machines
manager — a person who leads a team at work
office — a place where people do desk work
company — a business that people work for
salary — the money you earn for your job
meeting — when people come together to talk about work

## Functions & phrases
- Asking about jobs: "What do you do?" / "Where do you work?"
- Talking about your job: "I'm an engineer." / "I work for a big company."
- Asking about study: "What do you study?" / "Why do you learn English?"
- Saying dates: "It's the fifth of April." / "My class is on the ninth."

## Suggested activities
- reading: three short profiles of people describing their jobs and workplaces
- conversation: a job interview role-play with wh- questions about work and study
- quiz focus: word order in wh- questions, do/does, and reading dates with ordinals

## Watch out
- What you do? → What do you do?
- Where she works? → Where does she work?
- My birthday is the three of March → the third of March
