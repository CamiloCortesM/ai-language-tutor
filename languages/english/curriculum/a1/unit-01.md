# A1 · Unit 01 — Hello & introductions

**Can-do:** Can greet people, introduce myself and others, and give basic personal information using simple fixed phrases.

## Grammar focus
The verb *to be* (present) and subject pronouns. *To be* has three forms: **am** (I), **is** (he/she/it), **are** (you/we/they). Make negatives with **not** (I am not, she is not) and questions by putting the verb first (Are you…? Is he…?). Short forms are normal in speech: *I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're; isn't, aren't.* Subject pronouns replace the person's name and come before the verb.

- I am from Canada. / I'm a student.
- She is my teacher. / He's here.
- We are friends.
- You are not late. / You aren't late.
- They are not new students.
- Is she your friend? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
- Are you okay? — Yes, I am.
- Where are you from? — I'm from Mexico.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
hello — a friendly word you say when you meet someone
goodbye — a word you say when you leave
name — the word people call you
student — a person who studies
teacher — a person who teaches
friend — a person you like and know well
please — a polite word when you ask for something
thanks — a short way to say thank you
yes — a word that means agreement
no — a word that means refusal or "not"
sorry — a word you say when you make a mistake
nice — good or pleasant

## Functions & phrases
- Greeting: "Hello!" / "Hi, how are you?" / "Good morning."
- Introducing yourself: "My name is Ana." / "I'm Ana." / "Nice to meet you."
- Introducing others: "This is my friend Tom." / "Her name is Sara."
- Asking for basic details: "What's your name?" / "Where are you from?"
- Saying goodbye: "Goodbye!" / "See you later." / "Bye!"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a very short dialogue where two people meet and exchange names and countries
- conversation: role-play meeting a new classmate on the first day of school
- quiz focus: correct form of *to be* (am/is/are), matching pronouns to verbs, and choosing the right greeting

## Watch out
- I is a student → I am a student
- She are my friend → She is my friend
- You is late → You are late
