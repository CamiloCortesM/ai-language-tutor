# A1 · Unit 04 — Daily routines & time

**Can-do:** Can describe my daily routine, say how often I do things, and tell the time.

## Grammar focus
Present simple for habits and routines. Use the base verb for I/you/we/they and add **-s** (or **-es**) for he/she/it: *I work / she works, I go / he goes.* Negatives use **don't** / **doesn't** + base verb: *I don't work, she doesn't work.* Questions use **do** / **does** + base verb: *Do you work? Does he sleep?* Adverbs of frequency (**always, usually, often, sometimes, never**) go before the main verb but after *to be*. Tell the time with *It's…*: *It's seven o'clock. It's half past six.*

- I get up at seven o'clock.
- She works every day.
- We don't have breakfast late.
- He doesn't sleep in the afternoon.
- Do you work on Sundays? — No, I don't.
- Does she start early? — Yes, she does.
- I always drink coffee in the morning.
- What time is it? — It's half past eight.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
wake up — to stop sleeping
get up — to leave your bed
breakfast — the first meal of the day
lunch — the meal in the middle of the day
dinner — the evening meal
sleep — to rest with your eyes closed at night
morning — the early part of the day
afternoon — the part of the day after midday
evening — the part of the day before night
night — the dark part of the day
week — seven days
o'clock — a word for telling the full hour

## Functions & phrases
- Describing routine: "I get up at six." / "She works in the morning."
- Saying how often: "I always have breakfast." / "We never work at night."
- Telling the time: "It's nine o'clock." / "It's quarter past ten."
- Asking about routine: "What time do you get up?" / "When do you have lunch?"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short paragraph describing one person's typical day from morning to night
- conversation: interview a partner about their daily routine and times
- quiz focus: third-person -s, do/does questions and negatives, and telling the time

## Watch out
- She work every day → She works every day
- I don't works on Monday → I don't work on Monday
- What time is the clock? → What time is it?
