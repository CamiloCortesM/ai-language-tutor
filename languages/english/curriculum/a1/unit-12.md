# A1 · Unit 12 — Plans & review

**Can-do:** Can talk about simple future plans using going to, and use the full range of A1 grammar to communicate about myself and my world.

## Grammar focus
*Going to* for plans, plus a review of all A1 structures. Form future plans with **am/is/are + going to + base verb**: *I am going to travel. She is going to visit her family.* Use it for plans and intentions you have already decided. Negatives add **not** (*isn't going to*); questions put *be* first (*Are you going to…?*). This unit also recycles everything from Units 1–11: *to be*, *have got*, present simple and continuous, *there is/are*, *can*, and *was/were*.

- I am going to travel next week.
- She is going to visit her grandmother.
- We are going to meet on Saturday.
- He isn't going to work tomorrow.
- Are you going to come to the party? — Yes, I am.
- What are you going to do at the weekend?
- They're going to buy tickets soon.
- I'm going to be ready in five minutes.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
tomorrow — the day after today
next — coming soon after now (next week)
plan — an idea about what you will do
visit — to go and see a person or place
travel — to go from one place to another
meet — to come together with someone
party — a fun event with people, food and music
weekend — Saturday and Sunday
soon — in a short time from now
ticket — a paper that lets you travel or enter
trip — a short journey to a place
ready — prepared and able to start

## Functions & phrases
- Talking about plans: "I'm going to travel." / "We're going to meet on Sunday."
- Asking about plans: "What are you going to do?" / "Are you going to come?"
- Arranging to meet: "Let's meet at six." / "See you at the weekend."
- Reviewing self-description: "I'm…, I have got…, I can…, I like…, I was…"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short message about someone's plans for the weekend or a trip
- conversation: make plans with a partner for the weekend using going to
- quiz focus: **A1 checkpoint** covering Units 1–11 — to be, have got, present simple, present continuous, there is/are, can, was/were, articles, plurals, prepositions — plus this unit's going to

## Watch out
- I go to travel tomorrow → I'm going to travel tomorrow
- She going to visit → She is going to visit
- Are you going come? → Are you going to come?
