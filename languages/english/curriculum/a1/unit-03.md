# A1 · Unit 03 — Home & things

**Can-do:** Can describe my home and the things in a room, saying what there is and pointing to objects near and far.

## Grammar focus
*There is / there are* and the articles *a/an/the*; demonstratives *this/that/these/those*. Use **there is** (there's) for one thing and **there are** for more than one: *There is a table. There are two chairs.* Negatives: *there isn't / there aren't.* Use **a** before a consonant sound, **an** before a vowel sound, and **the** for something specific or already known. **This/these** point to things near you; **that/those** to things far from you (this = one near, these = many near, that = one far, those = many far).

- There is a kitchen and a bathroom.
- There are three bedrooms in my house.
- There isn't a garden.
- Are there any chairs? — Yes, there are.
- I live in an old house.
- This is my room and that is the kitchen.
- These books are mine; those books are yours.
- The door is open.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
house — a building where people live
room — a space inside a house with walls
kitchen — the room where you cook
bedroom — the room where you sleep
door — you open it to enter a room
window — glass in the wall that lets in light
table — flat furniture you put things on
chair — furniture you sit on
bed — furniture you sleep on
wall — the side of a room
floor — the bottom surface you walk on
garden — the outside area with plants

## Functions & phrases
- Describing a home: "There are two bedrooms." / "My house is small."
- Asking about a place: "Is there a kitchen?" / "How many rooms are there?"
- Pointing to things: "This is my bed." / "Those are the windows."
- Locating a room: "The bathroom is here." / "That is the living room."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short description of a small apartment and its rooms
- conversation: give a partner a tour of your home, naming rooms and furniture
- quiz focus: there is vs. there are, a/an choice, and this/that/these/those

## Watch out
- There is two rooms → There are two rooms
- I have a apple in the kitchen → an apple
- This are my books → These are my books
