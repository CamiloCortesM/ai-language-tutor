# A1 · Unit 09 — Shopping & money

**Can-do:** Can ask about prices, buy simple things in a shop, and talk about how much things cost.

## Grammar focus
*How much* for prices, a review of demonstratives, and a first look at countable vs. uncountable nouns. Ask prices with **How much is…? / How much are…?**: *How much is this bag? How much are these shoes?* Countable nouns can be counted and have plurals (*one apple, two apples*); uncountable nouns cannot be counted directly and have no plural (*money, water, sugar*) — use *how much* with uncountable and *how many* with countable: *How much money? How many bags?* Demonstratives point to what you want to buy: *I'd like that one.*

- How much is this coat?
- How much are those bags?
- It's ten dollars.
- They're twenty dollars.
- That's too expensive.
- I'd like this one, please.
- How much money do you have?
- How many bags would you like?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
money — coins and notes you pay with
price — how much something costs
cheap — not expensive; low price
expensive — costing a lot of money
buy — to get something by paying
sell — to give something for money
customer — a person who buys in a shop
dollar — a unit of money in some countries
cent — a small unit of money; part of a dollar
bag — a container you carry things in
change — the money you get back after paying
receipt — a paper that shows what you paid

## Functions & phrases
- Asking prices: "How much is this?" / "How much are these shoes?"
- Buying: "I'd like this bag, please." / "Can I pay now?"
- Reacting to price: "That's cheap." / "It's too expensive for me."
- At the till: "Here's your change." / "Can I have a receipt?"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short shop dialogue with prices and items for sale
- conversation: role-play buying clothes; ask prices and pay for two items
- quiz focus: how much vs. how many, is/are with prices, and countable vs. uncountable nouns

## Watch out
- How much are this bag? → How much is this bag?
- How many money do you have? → How much money do you have?
- It's very expensive too → It's too expensive
