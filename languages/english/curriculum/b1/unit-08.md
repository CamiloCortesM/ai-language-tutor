# B1 · Unit 08 — It was made here

**Can-do:** I can describe processes and facts where the action matters more than who does it.

## Grammar focus
The **passive voice** puts the important thing — the object of the action — at the front of the sentence, when who does the action is unknown, obvious or unimportant. Form it with **be + past participle**. Present simple passive: *am/is/are + past participle* (*Cars are made in this factory*). Past simple passive: *was/were + past participle* (*The bridge was built in 1890*). If you need to name the doer, add **by** (*The lamp was invented by Edison*), but often you leave it out. The passive is common in news, science and descriptions of how things are produced.

- Millions of phones **are sold** every year.
- This software **is used** by schools everywhere.
- The results **were announced** yesterday.
- Paper **is made** from wood.
- The factory **was opened** in 1975.
- These products **aren't tested** on animals.
- **Was** the message **sent** to everyone?
- The old machines **were replaced** last year.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
manufacture — to make goods in large amounts in a factory
factory — a building where goods are made by machines
produce — to make or grow something
invent — to create something that did not exist before
device — a small machine made for a particular purpose
material — the substance something is made from
supply — to provide something that is needed
export — to sell goods to another country
waste — material that is thrown away as useless
process — a series of steps to make or do something
carry out — to do or complete a task
set up — to start or establish something

## Functions & phrases
- Describing how things are made: "It's produced in…", "They're assembled by hand."
- Stating facts impersonally: "It is said that…", "The product is known for…"
- Explaining a process: "First the material is collected, then it's cleaned…"
- Talking about origin: "Where was it made?", "It was designed in Japan."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short "how it's made" text describing a process in the passive.
- conversation: describe how an everyday object is produced, using present-simple passives.
- quiz focus: turning active sentences into passive (present and past simple), with and without *by*.

## Watch out
- This car makes in Germany → This car is made in Germany
- The house was build in 1990 → The house was built in 1990
- The letter was wrote by my boss → The letter was written by my boss
