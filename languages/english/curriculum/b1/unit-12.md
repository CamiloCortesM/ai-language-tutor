# B1 · Unit 12 — Milestone

**Can-do:** I can combine the grammar and vocabulary of B1 to handle a PET-style checkpoint covering everything so far.

## Grammar focus
This is a review unit — no new grammar, just pulling Units 1–11 together the way a **B1 Preliminary (PET)** paper mixes them. Keep the key contrasts clear: present perfect vs past simple for finished vs unfinished time (U1); present perfect continuous with *for/since* for ongoing actions (U2); past perfect for the "past before the past" in narratives (U3); the right future form and the first conditional for real plans (U4) against the second conditional for imaginary ones (U5); *used to* for changed past habits (U6); reported speech with *say/tell* (U7); the passive for processes (U8); modals of deduction and question tags for guessing and checking (U9); defining relative clauses for identifying (U10); and verb + gerund/infinitive patterns with *so/such* (U11).

- I'**ve worked** here for a year; I **started** last July. *(U1–U2)*
- By the time I **called**, she'**d** already **left**. *(U3)*
- If I **had** more time, I'**d learn** to paint. *(U5)*
- I **used to** cycle, but now I **drive**. *(U6)*
- He **said** he **would** send the report. *(U7)*
- These bikes **are made** locally. *(U8)*
- The keys are gone — someone **must** have taken them. *(U9)*
- She's the teacher **who** changed my life. *(U10)*

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
review — to look at something again to check or study it
progress — the movement toward a better or finished state
achieve — to succeed in doing something after effort
confident — sure of your own ability
mistake — something done wrongly
improve — to become or make better
fluent — able to speak a language easily and well
challenge — something difficult that tests your ability
goal — something you want to achieve
feedback — advice about how well you are doing
brush up — to improve a skill you have not used recently
get by — to manage with what you have

## Functions & phrases
- Reflecting on progress: "I've come a long way.", "I'm more confident now."
- Talking about goals: "My next goal is…", "I'd like to reach B2."
- Handling exam tasks: "Let me think…", "Could you repeat that, please?"
- Giving and taking advice: "You could try…", "That's good feedback."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a mixed-grammar text (a personal blog) with comprehension questions in PET style.
- conversation: a paired PET-style task — discuss and agree on a plan, then describe a photo.
- quiz focus: a checkpoint covering Units 1–11 — tense contrasts, conditionals, passive, reported speech, relatives and verb patterns.

## Watch out
- I have started here last year → I started here last year
- If I would have time, I'd learn → If I had time, I'd learn
- She's the teacher which changed my life → She's the teacher who changed my life
