# B1 · Unit 11 — Patterns & connections

**Can-do:** I can use common verb patterns correctly and link my ideas naturally with everyday phrasal verbs.

## Grammar focus
Many verbs are followed by a fixed form. Some take the **gerund (-ing)**: *enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, keep, practise* (*I enjoy cooking*). Others take the **infinitive (to + verb)**: *want, decide, hope, promise, offer, learn, manage* (*I decided to leave*). A few change meaning with each (*stop to rest* = pause in order to rest; *stop resting* = end the resting). After a preposition, always use *-ing* (*good at drawing*). Use **so + adjective/adverb** and **such + (a) + noun** to add emphasis (*It was so cold; it was such a cold day*). This unit also builds a bank of high-frequency **phrasal verbs** for everyday connection.

- I **enjoy meeting** new people.
- She **decided to change** careers.
- We **kept trying** until it worked.
- He **offered to help** with the project.
- Do you **mind waiting** a moment?
- I'm **thinking of taking** up a hobby.
- It was **so** interesting that I lost track of time.
- It was **such a** good idea that everyone agreed.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
hobby — an activity you do for fun in your free time
avoid — to keep away from something
manage — to succeed in doing something difficult
suggest — to offer an idea or plan for others to consider
practise — to do something regularly to get better at it
deal with — to take action to handle a problem
get on with — to have a good relationship with someone
take up — to start a new activity or hobby
give in — to finally agree after refusing
look forward to — to feel happy about something coming
carry on — to continue doing something
end up — to reach a situation or place in the end

## Functions & phrases
- Talking about likes and choices: "I enjoy…", "I've decided to…"
- Making suggestions: "How about…?", "Why don't we…?"
- Linking ideas: "It was so good that…", "That's such a…"
- Talking about relationships and plans: "I get on well with…", "I'm looking forward to…"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short blog post about starting a new hobby, rich in verb patterns and phrasal verbs.
- conversation: tell a partner three things you enjoy doing and one you've decided to try.
- quiz focus: choosing gerund vs infinitive after common verbs, and so vs such.

## Watch out
- I enjoy to cook → I enjoy cooking
- She decided leaving → She decided to leave
- It was so good day → It was such a good day
