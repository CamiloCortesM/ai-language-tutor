# B1 · Unit 07 — He said, she said

**Can-do:** I can report what other people said, and use *say* and *tell* correctly.

## Grammar focus
**Reported (indirect) speech** tells what someone said without their exact words. When the reporting verb is in the past (*said, told*), the reported verb usually shifts one tense back: present simple → past simple, present continuous → past continuous, past simple → past perfect, *will* → *would*, *can* → *could*. Pronouns and time/place words also change (*I → he, today → that day, here → there*). The key verb choice: **tell** needs a person after it (*She told me she was tired*), while **say** does not take a person object (*She said she was tired*). *That* is optional after both.

- "I'm busy." → She **said** (that) she **was** busy.
- "We'll call you." → They **told** me they **would** call me.
- "I saw it." → He **said** he **had seen** it.
- "I can't come." → She **told** us she **couldn't** come.
- "The news is on." → He **said** the news **was** on.
- "I'm reading it now." → She **said** she **was reading** it **then**.
- "I live here." → He **said** he **lived** there.
- "I'll post it today." → She **said** she **would** post it **that day**.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
journalist — a person who writes news for papers, TV or online
headline — the title of a news story in large letters
rumour — a story people tell that may not be true
announce — to tell people about something officially
interview — a meeting where someone is asked questions
broadcast — to send out a programme on TV or radio
article — a piece of writing in a paper or magazine
source — a person or place that gives information
claim — to say something is true without proof
deny — to say that something is not true
point out — to tell someone a fact they may not know
find out — to learn something you did not know before

## Functions & phrases
- Reporting news: "She said (that)…", "He told me…"
- Passing on messages: "They asked me to tell you…", "Apparently, …"
- Checking a story: "Who told you that?", "Where did you hear it?"
- Being careful with rumours: "Supposedly…", "I heard that…, but I'm not sure."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short news report that quotes people, then a version that reports their words indirectly.
- conversation: play "message chain" — hear a sentence and report it to the next person with said/told.
- quiz focus: say vs tell, and shifting tenses and pronouns in reported statements.

## Watch out
- She said me she was tired → She told me she was tired / She said she was tired
- He told that he was busy → He said that he was busy / He told me he was busy
- She said she will call → She said she would call
