# B1 · Unit 09 — Reading between the lines

**Can-do:** I can say how sure I am about something based on evidence, and check information with question tags.

## Grammar focus
Use **modals of deduction** to guess about the present from evidence. **Must be** shows you are almost certain something is true (*The lights are on — they must be home*); **can't be** shows you are almost certain it is not true (*He can't be forty, he looks so young*). **Might, may** and **could** show possibility — you are not sure (*She might be at work; it could be a mistake*). All are followed by the bare infinitive (usually *be* + noun/adjective/*-ing*). **Question tags** are short questions added to the end of a statement to check or invite agreement: a positive statement takes a negative tag (*It's late, isn't it?*), and a negative statement takes a positive tag (*You don't smoke, do you?*).

- She isn't answering — she **must be** busy.
- That **can't be** right; check it again.
- He **might be** stuck in traffic.
- They **could be** telling the truth.
- This **may be** the wrong address.
- You're coming, **aren't** you?
- She doesn't drive, **does** she?
- It's been a long day, **hasn't** it?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
evidence — facts that show something is true
obvious — very easy to see or understand
guess — to give an answer you are not sure about
suspect — to think something is probably true
clue — a piece of information that helps solve a puzzle
sure — having no doubt about something
doubt — a feeling of not being certain
seem — to appear to be a certain way
notice — to see or become aware of something
assume — to think something is true without checking
rule out — to decide that something is not possible
figure out — to understand something after thinking about it

## Functions & phrases
- Making deductions: "It must be…", "That can't be…"
- Expressing uncertainty: "It might be…", "I'm not sure, but…"
- Checking information: "…, isn't it?", "…, don't you?"
- Reasoning aloud: "Judging by…, I'd say…"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short mystery where readers guess answers using must/might/can't be.
- conversation: look at photos and guess who the people are and what they're doing.
- quiz focus: choosing must/might/can't be, and completing question tags.

## Watch out
- He must to be tired → He must be tired
- She can't is right → She can't be right
- You are ready, isn't it? → You are ready, aren't you?
