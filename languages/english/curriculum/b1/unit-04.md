# B1 · Unit 04 — Real possibilities

**Can-do:** I can talk about future plans, predictions and likely results, and choose the right future form.

## Grammar focus
This unit reviews the **first conditional** (*If + present simple, … will + verb*) for real, likely future situations (*If it rains, we'll stay in*) and sorts out the main future forms. Use **will** for predictions, decisions made now and offers (*I'll help you*); **be going to** for plans and intentions already decided, and for predictions based on evidence (*Look at those clouds — it's going to rain*); the **present continuous** for fixed arrangements with a time and place (*I'm meeting them at six*). A first look at the **future continuous** (*will be + -ing*) describes an action in progress at a future moment (*This time tomorrow I'll be flying home*). *Unless* means "if not".

- If we **recycle** more, we **'ll cut** waste.
- I think temperatures **will rise** over the next century.
- Look at the sky — it'**s going to** pour.
- We'**re meeting** the volunteers **at** nine tomorrow.
- This time next week I'**ll be lying** on a beach.
- **Unless** we act now, the problem **will** get worse.
- She'**s going to** plant trees this weekend.
- Don't worry — I'**ll carry** that for you.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
forecast — a statement about what the weather or future will be
climate — the usual weather in a place over a long time
pollution — harmful substances in the air, water or land
recycle — to treat used things so they can be used again
opportunity — a good chance to do something
arrange — to plan and organise something in advance
prevent — to stop something from happening
likely — probably going to happen
plan ahead — to think about and prepare for the future
warn — to tell someone about a possible danger
sort out — to deal with a problem successfully
run out — to have no more of something left

## Functions & phrases
- Predicting: "I reckon it'll…", "There's a good chance that…"
- Talking about plans: "I'm planning to…", "We're going to…"
- Making offers and decisions: "I'll get it.", "Right, I'll do it now."
- Talking about conditions: "If that happens, we'll…", "Unless we hurry, we'll be late."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short article predicting how a town will change, mixing will and going to.
- conversation: plan a weekend project together, using arrangements ("I'm meeting…") and offers ("I'll bring…").
- quiz focus: choosing will / going to / present continuous, and completing first-conditional sentences.

## Watch out
- If it will rain, we will stay home → If it rains, we will stay home
- I will meet my friends at 6 (fixed plan) → I'm meeting my friends at 6
- This time tomorrow I fly to Rome → This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Rome
