# B2 · Unit 06 — Hindsight

**Can-do:** I can criticise, deduce and speculate about past actions using perfect modals.

## Grammar focus
Perfect modals talk about the past with *modal + have + past participle*. *should / shouldn't have* criticises or regrets a past action ("You **should have called**"). *must have* is a confident past deduction ("She **must have left** already"), and *can't / couldn't have* is its opposite, an impossibility ("He **can't have known**"). *might / may / could have* express a past possibility you are unsure about ("They **might have missed** the turn"). *needn't have + past participle* means you did something that was not necessary. The *have* never changes form after the modal, whatever the subject.

- You **should have booked** the tickets earlier.
- I **shouldn't have eaten** so much.
- The keys aren't here — I **must have dropped** them.
- She **can't have finished** already; it's too soon.
- They **might have got** stuck in traffic.
- He **could have warned** us about the delay.
- We **needn't have worried** — everything was fine.
- **Might** she **have forgotten** the meeting?

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
assume — to accept something as true without checking
blame — to say someone is responsible for something bad
warn — to tell someone about a possible danger or problem
deserve — to have earned something by your actions
negligent — failing to take proper care over something
foolproof — designed so that nothing can go wrong
avoidable — able to have been prevented
liable — legally responsible for something
oversight — a mistake caused by not noticing something
precaution — an action taken in advance to prevent harm
culprit — the person or thing responsible for a problem
verdict — an official decision or judgement

## Functions & phrases
- Criticising in hindsight: "You should never have signed it."
- Deducing about the past: "It must have been a mistake."
- Speculating cautiously: "She may well have misunderstood the instructions."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short "what went wrong" account inviting judgement of past choices.
- conversation: discuss a news mishap — what people should or shouldn't have done.
- quiz focus: choosing *must / can't / might have* for deduction vs *should have* for criticism.

## Watch out
- You should called me → You should have called me
- He must knew the truth → He must have known the truth
- She can't have went there → She can't have gone there
- I should have to study more → I should have studied more
