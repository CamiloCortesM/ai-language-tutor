# B2 · Unit 05 — Getting things done

**Can-do:** I can talk about services and jobs done for me by someone else, using the causative *have / get something done*.

## Grammar focus
Use the causative when someone else does a job for you: *have + object + past participle* ("I **had my car repaired**") — the mechanic did it, not you. *get* is slightly more informal ("I **got my hair cut**"). Keep the object between the verb and the past participle. It works in any tense ("She **is having** a house **built**", "We **'ll get** the report **printed**"). A second use expresses something unpleasant that happened to you ("They **had their flight cancelled**"). To focus on the person you arrange, use *get someone to do* or *have someone do*.

- I **had my teeth checked** last week.
- We're **getting the kitchen redecorated** at the moment.
- She **had her passport stolen** on holiday.
- You should **get that mole looked at**.
- They **had a new roof put on** before winter.
- I need to **have these documents translated**.
- I finally **got the plumber to fix** the leak.
- He **had the waiter bring** the bill.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
renovate — to repair and improve a building or room
install — to put equipment in place so it is ready to use
maintenance — the work of keeping something in good condition
outsource — to pay another company to do work for you
estimate — a rough calculation of a job's cost before it starts
warranty — a promise to repair or replace a faulty product
tailor — to make or adjust something for a specific need
convenient — easy and useful; suiting your needs
reliable — able to be trusted to work or behave well
premises — the building and land that a business occupies
refurbish — to clean and redecorate to make like new
supplier — a company that provides goods or services

## Functions & phrases
- Arranging services: "I'd like to have it delivered.", "Can I get this gift-wrapped?"
- Complaining about a service: "I had my order cancelled without warning."
- Recommending: "You should get the boiler serviced every year."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a customer review describing work they had done on their home.
- conversation: role-play booking a service — a haircut, a repair, a delivery.
- quiz focus: word order in *have / get + object + past participle* across different tenses.

## Watch out
- I cut my hair yesterday (a barber did it) → I had my hair cut yesterday
- I had repaired my car (a garage did it) → I had my car repaired
- She got painted the house → She got the house painted
- We had installed a new boiler (a firm did it) → We had a new boiler installed
