# B2 · Unit 07 — Reporting everything

**Can-do:** I can report statements, questions and commands accurately, and use precise reporting verbs.

## Grammar focus
In reported speech, tenses usually shift back one step (*present → past*, *will → would*, *can → could*), and pronouns and time words change ("**tomorrow**" → "**the next day**"). **Reported questions** use statement word order with no auxiliary ("Where do you live?" → "She asked where I **lived**"); yes/no questions take *if / whether*. **Reported commands** use *tell / ask someone + to-infinitive* ("Wait!" → "He told me **to wait**"). Many **reporting verbs** carry meaning and take fixed patterns: *suggest + -ing*, *admit + -ing*, *refuse + to*, *offer + to*, *warn someone + to*, *accuse someone of + -ing*.

- "I'm tired." → She said she **was** tired.
- "Will you help?" → He asked **whether I would help**.
- "Don't touch it." → They warned us **not to touch** it.
- "It was me." → He **admitted breaking** the window.
- "Let's take a break." → She **suggested taking** a break.
- "No, I won't go." → He **refused to go**.
- "You stole it." → She **accused him of stealing** it.
- "I'll drive you." → He **offered to drive** me.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
claim — to say something is true without proof
deny — to say that something is not true
insist — to say firmly that something must happen or is true
admit — to agree that something bad is true
suggest — to put forward an idea for consideration
recommend — to advise a particular action as a good one
threaten — to say you will cause harm if a demand is not met
persuade — to make someone agree by giving reasons
apologise — to express regret for a fault
promise — to say firmly that you will do something
announce — to state something officially and publicly
complain — to say that you are unhappy about something

## Functions & phrases
- Relaying news: "She mentioned that…", "Apparently, they said…"
- Reporting a request: "He asked me to…", "They told us not to…"
- Summarising a claim: "He denied any involvement in the affair."

## Suggested activities
- reading: an interview transcript to convert into a reported summary.
- conversation: play "telephone" — pass on statements, questions and orders in turn.
- quiz focus: backshift, reported-question word order, and reporting-verb patterns.

## Watch out
- She said me that she was busy → She told me that she was busy
- He asked where do I live → He asked where I lived
- They told to wait → They told us to wait
- He suggested me to leave → He suggested that I leave / He suggested leaving
