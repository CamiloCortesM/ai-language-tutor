# B2 · Unit 11 — Word power

**Can-do:** I can build new words with prefixes and suffixes, and choose natural word partnerships.

## Grammar focus
English builds words with **affixes**. Prefixes change meaning (*un-, dis-, mis-, over-, under-, re-, ir-*) while suffixes change word class: *-tion, -ment, -ness, -ity* make nouns; *-ise / -ize, -en* make verbs; *-al, -ous, -ful, -less, -able* make adjectives; *-ly* makes adverbs. **Collocations** are words that naturally go together (*make a decision*, *heavy rain*, *strong argument* — not *do a decision*). **Phrasal verbs** often form families around one particle (*put off, put up with, put forward*); some are separable, some inseparable, and the meaning is rarely literal, so learn them as whole units.

- *possible → impossibility*; *nation → nationalise*; *care → careless → carelessly*.
- The government failed to **take action** on **rising unemployment**.
- Please **carry out** the survey and **point out** any errors.
- They had to **call off** the launch and **look into** the fault.
- Her **misunderstanding** led to an **overreaction**.
- We should **draw up** a plan and **set out** the goals clearly.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
implement — to put a plan or decision into action
sustainability — the ability to continue without using up resources
accountability — being responsible and having to explain your actions
innovative — introducing new ideas or methods
overestimate — to think something is bigger or better than it is
downturn — a decline in economic or business activity
streamline — to make a process simpler and more efficient
undertake — to take on and begin a task or responsibility
disregard — to ignore or pay no attention to something
feasible — possible to do easily or conveniently
counterproductive — having the opposite of the desired effect
turnover — the total amount of business done in a period

## Functions & phrases
- Nominalising for formality: "the implementation of the plan", "a lack of accountability"
- Using collocations: "reach an agreement", "meet a deadline", "raise awareness"
- Managing tasks with phrasal verbs: "sort out", "deal with", "follow up on"

## Suggested activities
- reading: a business article to mine for collocations and phrasal verbs.
- conversation: describe a project using word families (verb → noun → adjective).
- quiz focus: word formation (put the root word in the right form) and verb-noun collocations.

## Watch out
- do a decision → make a decision
- make a research → do research / carry out research
- We cancelled off the meeting → We called off the meeting
- an unresponsible manager → an irresponsible manager
