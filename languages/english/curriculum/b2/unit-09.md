# B2 · Unit 09 — The future, completed

**Can-do:** I can talk about actions in progress at a future time and actions finished by a future point.

## Grammar focus
The **future continuous** (*will be + -ing*) describes an action in progress at a specific future moment ("This time tomorrow I **'ll be flying** to Tokyo") or a planned, expected future event. The **future perfect** (*will have + past participle*) describes something completed before a future point ("By 2030 the company **will have doubled** in size"); it often pairs with *by*, *by the time* or *by then*. Both are common in predictions about technology and the environment. Add *will have been + -ing* (future perfect continuous) when you want to stress the duration up to that future point.

- Don't call at eight — I **'ll be having** dinner.
- By next month, we **'ll have finished** the prototype.
- This time next year she **'ll be studying** abroad.
- By the time you arrive, they **'ll have left**.
- In a decade, AI **will have transformed** most industries.
- **Will** you **be using** the car tonight?
- By June, I **'ll have been working** here for ten years.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
automate — to make a process work by machines instead of people
sustainable — able to continue without harming the environment
renewable — from a source that is not used up, like sun or wind
emissions — gases released into the air, especially harmful ones
breakthrough — an important, sudden advance or discovery
obsolete — no longer used because something better exists
disruptive — causing radical change to an existing market or way of doing things
efficiency — working well with little waste of time or energy
forecast — a statement about what is likely to happen
deploy — to bring something into use or action
scalable — able to grow in size or output easily
transition — the process of changing from one state to another

## Functions & phrases
- Predicting completion: "By then, we'll have solved most of the bugs."
- Describing future routine: "I'll be working from home next week."
- Projecting trends: "Experts forecast that emissions will have fallen by 2040."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a "world in 2040" article using future perfect and future continuous.
- conversation: predict your life and technology five years from now.
- quiz focus: choosing future continuous vs future perfect from time markers (*at* vs *by*).

## Watch out
- By 2030 we will finish the project → By 2030 we will have finished the project
- This time tomorrow I will fly → This time tomorrow I will be flying
- By the time she comes, we will leave → By the time she comes, we will have left
- I will have finish it → I will have finished it
