# B2 · Unit 04 — It is said that…

**Can-do:** I can report information impersonally when the source is unknown or unimportant, using the passive in any tense.

## Grammar focus
The passive moves the focus onto the action or its receiver: *be + past participle*, with the tense carried by *be* ("The results **are being checked**", "The bridge **had been built** by 1900"). Use it when the doer is unknown, obvious, or less important than the result. **Passive reporting structures** distance you from a claim: *It is said / believed / thought that…* + clause, or *subject + is said / thought + to-infinitive* ("He **is believed to be** abroad"; "The painting **is thought to have been** stolen"). These forms are common in news and academic writing, where the source is often vague.

- The vaccine **is being tested** in several countries.
- Millions of trees **had been planted** before the scheme ended.
- The suspect **will be questioned** tomorrow.
- The documents **should have been shredded**.
- **It is believed that** the fire started in the kitchen.
- The CEO **is said to be** considering resignation.
- The manuscript **is thought to have been written** in the 1400s.
- Very little **is known** about the author's early life.

## Target vocabulary (10–12 items)
allege — to say something is true without providing proof
source — a person or document that supplies information
confirm — to state that something is definitely true
estimate — to guess an amount using available information
reportedly — according to what people say
undergo — to experience a process or treatment
findings — the information discovered by research or investigation
widespread — happening over a large area or among many people
credible — able to be believed; convincing
speculation — guessing about something without firm evidence
allegedly — as claimed, but not proven
verify — to check that something is true or accurate

## Functions & phrases
- Reporting impersonally: "It is widely believed that…", "It has been reported that…"
- Distancing yourself: "The company is said to…", "Reportedly, …"
- Hedging a claim: "There is speculation that a merger is planned."

## Suggested activities
- reading: a short news report full of passive and passive-reporting forms.
- conversation: present a "breaking story" using impersonal passives, then react to it.
- quiz focus: turning active reports into *It is said that…* and *is said to…* structures.

## Watch out
- The house is build in 1950 → The house was built in 1950
- It is said the prices to rise → It is said that prices will rise / Prices are said to rise
- People say that he is rich → He is said to be rich
- The letter was wrote by the manager → The letter was written by the manager
