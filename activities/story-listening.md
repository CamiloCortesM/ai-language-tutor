# Activity: Story listening

**Mode:** voice-required · **Availability:** Codex voice mode only · **Duration:** 8–12 min

The tutor tells a short, level-appropriate story aloud in the target language. The learner listens and answers questions aloud. It trains comprehension of natural speech without showing the story text first.

## Run it

1. Use Codex voice mode. If it is off, ask the learner to turn voice on. Outside Codex, do not offer this activity and do not use a bridge or JSON handoff.
2. Pick a topic from the current unit or the learner's interests. Keep the story at 95–98% known words and introduce at most 2–3 useful new words.
3. Tell the story once. A1–A2 may ask for one verbatim replay; B1+ gets one play unless they explicitly request a replay.
4. Ask three questions aloud: one gist question, one detail question, and one simple opinion or prediction question. Do not reveal the text until they answer.
5. Give brief feedback after all answers: comprehension first, then at most one correction that blocked meaning.

## Write-backs

Log missed known words as perception errors, unknown useful words as cards, and the activity in `progress.json` history as `story-listening`. If voice is unavailable, skip the activity; do not count a typed substitute as completed speaking/listening.
